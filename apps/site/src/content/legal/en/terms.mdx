## What the subscription covers

The subscription gives you the right to use the desktop app, the agent installed on your servers and the console, and to receive their updates. It is counted per managed server, paid monthly or yearly, and the prices shown on the pricing page are the ones that apply.

It does not cover the server, its hosting, your model provider subscriptions, or the licences of the third-party software the agent installs for you.

## What you bring

You bring the server, the host account that bills it, and the model subscriptions your agents use. You stay responsible for what runs on the machine, for the data it holds, and for the backups you keep of it.

Your private keys never leave your computer. We hold no copy and have no way of obtaining one.

## What we never do

No inbound connection to your server: your app calls out, never the other way round. No custody of your private keys. No support access to your machine — if support needs to see a log, it asks you to send it. Nothing readable dropped on the server: a binary and configuration files, nothing else.

## Trial and payment

Fourteen days of trial, without a card. Then a monthly or yearly subscription, cancellable at any time from the console, running until the end of the period you paid for.

Payments are collected by Stripe as merchant of record: Stripe invoices, collects the applicable taxes and holds the payment data. We never see a card number. [Refund policy to be settled before the service opens.]

## When the subscription ends

Your server keeps running. Installed services stay installed, your projects stay yours, nothing is erased.

The agent enters restricted mode seven days after the entitlement ends: it stops accepting commands from the app without stopping anything that runs. You lose the app, not the machine. Uninstalling the agent is documented and takes one command.
