## Your machine, your responsibility

The server is yours: we do not watch it and we do not read what is on it. This policy says what you undertake not to do with our tools, and what we do the day we learn about it through a report or through your host.

## Prohibited uses

Nothing that is illegal where you are and where the server is hosted. No intrusion, scanning or flooding of a system that is not yours. No malware, no collection of stolen data, no fraud, no harassment, no child sexual abuse material, and no content that exploits or endangers a person.

## Model providers

An agent you run stays subject to the terms of the provider whose model it calls. You do not use Pupitre to work around their limits, to share an account beyond what they allow, or to automate a use they forbid.

## Resource abuse

Cryptocurrency mining, account farms, unsolicited mail and anonymous traffic relaying are not allowed on a machine Pupitre manages: they get your server suspended by your host, and our name with it.

## Reports

Report abuse to `support@pupitre.studio`, with the address of the server and what you observed. We acknowledge the report and we answer the person who sent it. What we can see of a server is limited to what the report contains: we have no window onto your machine.

## What we can do

Depending on severity: write to you, suspend the entitlement of the server concerned, or end the subscription. We stop nothing on your machine, delete no data and cut off nothing that runs — the agent stops obeying the app, that is all. A suspension based on a mistake is lifted as soon as the mistake is established.
