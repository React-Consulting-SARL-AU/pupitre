## What is not on it

The data that lives on your machines and in your backups goes through none of them: it stays with the host of your machine and with the storage provider you chose, with whom you contract yourself. The model providers your coding agents call are yours, on your own subscriptions.

## Changes

Adding or replacing a sub-processor is announced by email to the owners of every organisation before it happens, then published here with its date. An organisation may object as the [data processing agreement](/legal/data-processing/) provides, by writing to `legal@pupitre.studio`.
