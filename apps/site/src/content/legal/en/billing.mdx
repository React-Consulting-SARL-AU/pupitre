These terms are part of the [terms of service](/legal/terms/). They apply from the day billing opens. During the free launch, nothing is paid, no card is asked for, and nothing below commits you to pay anything.

## When billing opens

The day billing opens is announced by email to every active account at least thirty days ahead. Nothing is ever charged without an explicit action on your part: you pay from the moment you subscribe, not from the moment the launch ends.

If your organisation enrolled a machine during the free launch, the seat the launch gave it stays free, as the [terms](/legal/terms/) and the [licence](/legal/licence/) say: one machine of that organisation is never billed.

## Who sells you the subscription

Payments are collected by Stripe as merchant of record: Stripe sells you the subscription, invoices you, collects the taxes and holds your payment data. The purchase terms Stripe shows you at checkout apply in addition to these; we remain responsible for the service itself. The way payments are collected and the payment provider may change, with the thirty days' notice the terms provide.

## The trial

An organisation that does not have a subscription yet can try Pupitre for free for thirty days, on one machine, without a card. At the end of the trial, nothing is charged: the subscription only starts if you subscribe. Without a subscription, the machine enters restricted mode as with any end of the right of use, and keeps running.

## The price

The price is per enrolled machine — one seat per machine — and per period, monthly or yearly, as the [pricing page](/pricing/) shows it on the day you subscribe. The taxes that apply where you are come on top of the price shown and are calculated by Stripe at checkout, which may also show you the price in your currency.

Enrolling a machine adds a seat, removing one frees it; the amount for the current period is adjusted pro rata, up or down, on the next invoice.

## Renewal

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the price in force, until you cancel it. A price increase is announced to you by email at least thirty days before it applies, and only affects the periods that start after that date: you can cancel until then.

## Failed payment

When a payment fails, Stripe retries and tells you. Your machines keep their right of use for seven days; after that, the organisation is suspended and its machines enter restricted mode until the payment goes through. Nothing is erased, neither on your machines nor on the platform.

## Cancellation

You cancel at any time, from the console's billing area, which opens Stripe's, or by writing to `support@pupitre.studio`. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid: your machines keep their right of use until then, then enter restricted mode and keep running. An account that holds a running subscription can only be deleted once it is cancelled.

## Refunds

Within fourteen days of the first payment of a subscription, and within fourteen days of the renewal of a yearly subscription, you can ask for a full refund of that payment, without giving a reason: write to `support@pupitre.studio` from the address of an owner of the organisation. The subscription then stops immediately.

Outside those cases, a period that has started is not refunded, except where the law requires it, when we discontinue the service before the end of what you paid for — the unused part is then refunded —, or when we charged you by mistake.

## Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the European Union, the United Kingdom or a country that provides a similar right, you have fourteen days after subscribing to withdraw, without giving a reason. The service starts as soon as you subscribe, at your request. The fourteen-day refund described above goes beyond what the law owes you: it gives you back the whole payment, including the days already used. To withdraw, write to `support@pupitre.studio` before the period ends, saying that you withdraw.

## Invoices

Invoices and receipts are issued by Stripe and sent to the billing address you give it. We keep your organisation's invoice history for as long as accounting law requires.
