This agreement is part of the [terms of service](/legal/terms/) and applies to every organisation from the moment it exists, without a signature. An organisation that needs a signed copy asks for it at `legal@pupitre.studio` and receives it back, signed.

## Parties and roles

The organisation is the controller of the personal data it brings to the platform. The processor is Jordan Monier, a natural person residing in Morocco, who publishes Pupitre. Where the applicable law calls those roles differently, the obligations below apply all the same.

## Scope

The agreement covers the personal data we process on behalf of your organisation when you use the console, the app and the platform: your members' accounts and sessions, their devices and the public halves of their keys, the journal of the organisation, and the metadata of your machines listed in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/).

It does not cover the data that lives on your machines. We have no access to it and no technical means of obtaining it: for that data, we are not your processor, and you are the only one at the controls.

## Instructions

We process that data only to provide the service, on your documented instructions — these terms and what you do in the console — and for nothing else. We do not sell it, we do not share it for anyone's own purposes, and we train no model on it. If an instruction seemed to us to break the law, we would tell you before acting on it.

## Confidentiality

The people who can read the platform's data are the members of the Pupitre organisation, each bound to confidentiality, and each reading of a sensitive thread is journaled.

## Security measures

Encryption in transit and at rest. Sign-in by link, passkey or second factor. Sessions bound to the device that opened them. Access to production systems limited to the people who need it, and journaled. Secrets kept out of the code, injected by a password manager. Agent distributed as a signed binary, verified before installation. No inbound connection to your machines, no command executed on them, no file read on them. Dependencies watched for published vulnerabilities and updated within the day for the ones that sign you in.

## Transfers

The platform's database and files are stored in Cloudflare's Western Europe region; the processor reads them from Morocco, and the sub-processors are established in the United States.

For an organisation established in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the transfer to the processor relies on the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission by decision 2021/914, module two (controller to processor), which this agreement incorporates by reference with the organisation as data exporter and the processor as data importer, together with the United Kingdom addendum where it applies. The transfer to each sub-processor relies on the clauses and guarantees that sub-processor publishes in its own data processing terms.

## Assistance and breaches

We help you answer requests from data subjects and meet the obligations that fall on you, within what the platform allows. We tell you without undue delay, and within seventy-two hours of learning it, about a personal data breach that affects your organisation, with what we know of its nature, its scope and the measures taken.

## Audits

The documents evidencing our measures are provided on request. Once a year, and on thirty days' notice, you or an auditor you mandate may verify them; the audit is at your cost and touches nothing that concerns another organisation.

## Deletion

When you delete your organisation, or your account with the organisations where you were the only member, its data is deleted at once; a deletion we perform at your request is scheduled seven days ahead, then carried out. Before that, an export of your organisation's data is handed to you on request. What accounting law will require us to keep, the day invoices exist, is kept for that reason and that duration only.

## Getting a signed copy

Write to `legal@pupitre.studio` from the address of an owner of the organisation. You receive this agreement, dated and signed, with the standard contractual clauses completed for your organisation.
