You pay per server, per month. The app and the agent come with it, on every device you use. See the [pricing](/pricing/) for the figures.

An account is required, and the app enrols a server only under a subscription: the free launch, the trial, or a paid one.

## The launch

Until billing opens, Pupitre is free: **Start** in the console opens a launch subscription for one machine per organisation, with no card. The pricing page gives the date the launch ends. An organisation that enrolled a machine during the launch keeps that seat for free for good. Billing opening will be announced by email thirty days ahead, and nothing is ever charged without a step of yours.

## The trial

Once billing is open: thirty days, on one machine, with no card. An organisation has one trial. When it ends nothing is deleted: the server keeps running, and you subscribe when you want the app back.

## Yearly

Two months are free on the yearly plan. The switch happens in the console; the app follows on its next check-in.

## What counts as a server

A machine enrolled with the platform, under your organisation. Deleting a server in the console frees its seat at once; the agent then takes the platform's keys out of the server and goes into restricted mode. See [uninstall](/docs/account/uninstall/).

## Payments

Checkout and the customer portal will be handled by Stripe as merchant of record. The site does not sell: the order button opens the console, and no card ever touches `pupitre.studio`.

## When it stops

The server keeps working as an ordinary server. Projects, databases, services and tunnels stay in place, and you connect over SSH as before.

- **A failed payment** opens seven days of grace: everything keeps working, and the console and the email say until when. After that, the servers are suspended.
- **A cancelled or ended subscription** suspends the servers at their next check-in with the platform.
- **A server that cannot reach the platform** keeps its last answer for seven days, then suspends itself.

A suspended server puts the agent in restricted mode: the app still reads its state and can update the agent, but installs and changes wait. Nothing that already runs is stopped. See [troubleshooting](/docs/account/troubleshooting/).

Subscribe again and the app picks up where it left off, at the server's next check-in.
