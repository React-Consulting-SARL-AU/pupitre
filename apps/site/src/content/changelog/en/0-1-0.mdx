The first build anyone outside the project can install. It does the whole onboarding: add a server, inspect it, choose services, configure, install, harden, first project.

## The app

- Servers, with one ed25519 key generated per device and an SSH configuration the app owns.
- The inspection screen: distribution, memory, disk, architecture, and what is already installed.
- The catalogue and its configuration screens, driven by the manifests the agent declares.
- Live install with the module, the step, the counter and the elapsed time, and the replay command on failure.
- Hardening, and the switch from root to `dev` — root closed last, after the key is verified.
- First project: clone, dependencies, `tmux`, port, URL, logs.
- The dashboard, and projects on the new protocol.

## The agent

- The probe, the module engine and the end-of-install report.
- The base and hardening modules, the runtimes, the databases, the AI agents, the remote editors, exposure and tools.
- The project registry, and driving `tmux` sessions, processes, captures, secrets and databases.

## The platform

- Accounts, devices, enrolment, agent tokens and heartbeat.
- Signed binary distribution, subscriptions, organisations, transactional email, alerts, passkeys.

## Known limits

- The app is beta. It is signed and notarised on macOS, signed on Windows, and an AppImage on Linux.
- Automatic updates are not on yet: this version is downloaded by hand.
- `Hosted` does not exist. You bring the server.
