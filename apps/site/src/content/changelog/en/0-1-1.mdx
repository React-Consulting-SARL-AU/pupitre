A small version, mostly around getting the app onto your computer.

## The console

- The download page recognises your machine: the system from the browser, the processor when the browser says it — an Apple silicon Mac is offered the arm64 build first, an Intel one the x64 build.
- Linux is listed twice, as it should be: the AppImage and the `.deb` for the same machine, each named by its format. Before, the second one was refused as a duplicate of the first.
- The release notes of a version are rendered as text, not shown as raw Markdown.
- When no stable version exists yet, the page offers the beta and says so.
- After confirming a device from the console, the page sends you back to the app.
- A banner on every page says that Pupitre is under development and that nothing is open to the public yet. The site carries the same banner.

## The app

- The sign-in screen no longer repeats that an account is needed.
- A platform answer that is not what the app expected is reported with its status, instead of a generic failure.

## The platform

- The database no longer sleeps between two requests: the first request after a quiet period answers as fast as the others.
- Versions now go out on the stable channel directly. The beta channel still exists, but nothing is published there.
