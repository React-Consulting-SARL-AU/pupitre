Two fixes on the path between the app and the platform, and two wording corrections.

## The app

- Checking for updates now finds them. The app asked for a feed file named after the channel — `stable-mac.yml` — which nobody publishes; it now reads `latest-mac.yml` from the channel folder, as it should.
- Without an account, the settings say what that prevents — no server can be installed or updated until an account is connected — instead of describing what a production build refuses.

## The platform

- Signing in from the app no longer fails at the device-code exchange. The platform answered that step with an error, so the app never got its confirmation.

## The console

- Once a device is confirmed, the button that sends you back to the app sits below the message, on its own line, instead of being squeezed beside it.

## Known limits

- An app already installed — 0.1.0 or 0.1.1 — carries the old feed name and cannot find this version by itself: install 0.1.2 from the download page. From there on, updates arrive on their own.
