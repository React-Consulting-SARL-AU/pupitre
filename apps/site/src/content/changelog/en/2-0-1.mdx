A fix in the app, and a consent asked of every account on the platform.

## The app

- **A project stuck starting can be stopped.** Until now, a project whose start never finished only offered **Start**, which did nothing since its process was already there: there was no way to stop it from the app. A project left starting, or failed with its process still open, now offers **Restart** and **Stop** — on the dashboard card, on the project page and on each of its processes.
- **Stop all** on the dashboard is no longer greyed out when the projects are only starting.
- When the platform asks for your consent before answering, the app says so and sends you to the console to give it, instead of an unexplained refusal.

## The console

- **Every account is asked to consent to its data going through Cloudflare**, on its first entry, whatever the way in. The sign-in page says where the data goes before the email is typed. Until the consent is given, the console and the app's requests to the platform are refused; declining erases the sign-in just created.
- The audience of the site and the console is now measured by Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies; PostHog and its consent banner are gone. Cloudflare is the only active sub-processor.

## The site

- The legal pages say plainly that everything is stored and processed by Cloudflare in the United States, a transfer outside Morocco and the European Union that rests on that consent.
- Each version now has its GitHub release, with links to every installer.

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is still unsigned: SmartScreen warns at first launch.
